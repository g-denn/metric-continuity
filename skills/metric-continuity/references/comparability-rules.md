# Comparability rules

A metric name alone is not a definition. Compare its stated definition, reporting scope, adjustment set, unit or currency, covered population, and treatment of acquisitions, divestitures, discontinued operations, and recasts.

Use `comparable` only when the disclosed bases are consistent. Use `comparable_after_issuer_recast` only when the issuer expressly places prior results on the later basis. Use `directional_only` only when a disclosed difference makes a rough directional reading reasonable but prohibits like-for-like comparison. Use `not_comparable` for a material basis difference. Use `unknown` where evidence is insufficient or ambiguity remains.

If issuer identity, metric, authorization/non-confidentiality, two usable dated sources, or a required date is missing, return `Research incomplete` and no classification. With sufficient evidence, unresolved basis ambiguity is `unknown`; an explicit material disclosed basis difference is `not_comparable`. Never normalize, restate, or create a recast on the issuer's behalf. Do not convert a missing definition into an assumed one.
