# Product vision

Metric Continuity helps research-oriented users avoid a quiet analytical error: treating a repeated metric name as a continuous series when the issuer changed its basis. Its user is an analyst, journalist, product builder, or investor doing source-first public research. The product organizes evidence; it does not recommend an action.

Version 0.1.0 is deliberately narrow: two or more dated public disclosures, one named KPI or non-GAAP measure, one continuity classification, and a transparent ledger. It will not ingest private data, generate recasts, or claim accounting or legal compliance.
